    About Job Scouts AI

    Job Scouts AI is a free, open source, browser-based AI job search tool. No backend, no server, no subscriptions. You bring your own free Google AI Studio API key and everything runs in a single HTML page.

    Released under the MIT License. Source code: github.com/DavidFliesen/jobscoutsai

    Privacy

    Your API key, settings, and profile live only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server except Google, when you run a scout or analyze a resume with your own key.

    Your use of the Gemini API is governed by the Google AI terms of service.

    How It Works

    This static page (hosted free on GitHub Pages) calls the Google Gemini API directly from your browser using your key. Your targets, skills, preferences, and optional resume profile are compiled into a structured prompt; Gemini returns scored job leads as JSON, rendered here as cards and exportable reports.
